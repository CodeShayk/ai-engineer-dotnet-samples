# Account and Privacy

## Updating your account

You can change your name, email address, delivery addresses and password from your Northwind account at any time.

## Your data

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to delete your account and data, from the privacy section of your account. We respond to requests within 30 days.

## Protecting your information

We only share information about an order with the account holder. Northwind support staff will never ask for your full card number or your password.
