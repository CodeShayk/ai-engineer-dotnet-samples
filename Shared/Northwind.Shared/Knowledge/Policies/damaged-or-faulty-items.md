# Damaged or Faulty Items

## Items damaged in transit

If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery from your Northwind account or by contacting our support team. Please include photos of the damage and of the packaging. You do not need to repackage the item in its original packaging.

## Faulty items

If an item stops working or develops a fault, contact us within the warranty period. We will arrange a free return and offer a replacement, a repair or a full refund.

## Return shipping

Returns of damaged or faulty items are always free. We will email you a prepaid label or arrange a collection for large items such as furniture.
