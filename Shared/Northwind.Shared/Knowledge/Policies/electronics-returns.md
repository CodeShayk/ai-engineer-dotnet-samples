# Electronics Returns

## Unopened electronics

Electronics that have not been opened can be returned within the standard 30-day return window, starting on the day of delivery.

## Opened electronics

Opened electronics can be returned within 14 days of delivery, as long as all accessories, cables, manuals and original packaging are included. This applies to items such as earbuds, speakers, watches and laptops. There is no restocking fee for opened electronics returned within this window.

After 14 days, opened electronics can no longer be returned for a refund, but they remain covered by the warranty if they develop a fault.

## Your personal data

Before returning a device, sign out of any accounts and reset it to factory settings. Northwind is not responsible for personal data left on returned devices.

## Faulty or damaged electronics

If an electronic item arrives damaged or stops working, see the Damaged or Faulty Items policy. Faulty items can be returned at any time within the warranty period.
