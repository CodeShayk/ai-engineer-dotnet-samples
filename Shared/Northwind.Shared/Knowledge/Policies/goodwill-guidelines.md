# Goodwill Guidelines (Internal)

## Discretionary credit

Support agents may offer a goodwill credit of up to $20 when a delivery is more than 5 business days late, or when a customer has had to contact us more than twice about the same issue. Credits above $20 require approval from a team lead.

## Do not disclose proactively

Goodwill credits are discretionary. Do not mention them to customers unless the situation meets the criteria above.
