# Northwind Rewards

## Earning points

Members earn one point for every dollar spent at Northwind. Points are added to your account when your order is delivered.

## Tiers

Members start at Bronze. Spending $500 in a calendar year moves you to Silver, which earns 25% more points. Spending $1,500 moves you to Gold, which earns 50% more points and includes free express delivery on every order.

## Using points

Every 100 points can be redeemed for $5 off a future order. Points expire after 18 months without account activity.
