# Price Match Promise

## How it works

If you find an identical item for a lower price at a major national retailer's own website within 14 days of your purchase, we will refund the difference to your original payment method. The item must be in stock and available to buy at the time you contact us.

## Exclusions

The price match promise does not apply to marketplace sellers, clearance or final sale prices, bundle deals, membership-only prices, or prices that include vouchers or cashback.

## How to claim

Contact our support team with your order number and a link to the lower price.
