# Refunds Policy

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid to your original payment method. If you paid with a Northwind gift card, the refund is issued as store credit.

## How long refunds take

You will receive a full refund to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse. Your bank may take a few additional days to show the refund on your statement.

Refunds for orders canceled before dispatch are issued within 3 to 5 business days of the cancellation.

## What is refunded

We refund the price you paid for the returned items. Original delivery charges are refunded only when the return is because an item was faulty, damaged or not what you ordered.

## Partial refunds

We may issue a partial refund if a returned item is damaged after delivery, has missing parts, or shows signs of use beyond what is needed to inspect it. We will always explain the reason for a partial refund by email.
