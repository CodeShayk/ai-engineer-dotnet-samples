# Returns Policy (2024)

## Our standard return window

Most items, including opened electronics, can be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. Items should be returned with their original packaging where possible.

## Items that cannot be returned

Groceries and personalized items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or faulty.

## How to start a return

Contact our support team by email with your order number and the items you want to return. We will send you a prepaid return label.

## After we receive your return

Refunds are issued within 14 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
