# Returns Policy

## Our standard return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The return window starts on the day the item is delivered, not the day you placed the order. Items should be unused and returned with their original tags and packaging where possible.

If you are not sure whether an item can be returned, check your order in your Northwind account, where each eligible item shows a Return an item button until its return window closes.

## Items that cannot be returned

Groceries, personalized items and products marked as final sale cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or faulty. Final sale items are clearly labeled on the product page and in your order confirmation.

Opened electronics have a shorter return window. See the Electronics Returns policy for details.

## How to start a return

Sign in to your account, open the order and choose Return an item. Select the item and the reason for the return, then print the prepaid label we email to you, or show the QR code at a participating drop-off point.

Returns are free when an item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered. For other returns, the cost of the return label is deducted from your refund.

## After we receive your return

We inspect returned items within two business days of arrival. Once the inspection is complete, we process your refund as described in the Refunds Policy. You will receive an email when your refund has been issued.
