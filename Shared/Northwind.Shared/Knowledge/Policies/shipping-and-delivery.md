# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days for orders placed before 2 pm local time. International delivery typically takes 7 to 14 business days, depending on the destination and customs.

Saturday delivery is available with Express delivery in selected areas. You can see whether it is available for your address at checkout.

## Tracking your order

We email you a tracking number as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also track your order from your Northwind account at any time.

## Delayed deliveries

If your estimated delivery date has passed, or the tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact our support team. We will investigate with the courier and keep you updated.

## Lost parcels

If the courier confirms that a parcel is lost, we will send a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer. Investigations usually take up to 10 business days.
