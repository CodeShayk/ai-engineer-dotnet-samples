# Warranty (United Kingdom)

## Extended warranty on electronics

For customers in the United Kingdom, electronics purchased from Northwind are covered by a two-year warranty from the date of delivery. This is in addition to your statutory rights under UK consumer law, which are not affected.

## Making a claim

Claims are handled in the same way as the standard warranty. Contact our support team with your order number and a description of the fault.
