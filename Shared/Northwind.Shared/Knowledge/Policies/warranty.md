# Warranty

## Standard warranty

Most products sold by Northwind come with a one-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, normal wear and tear, misuse, or modifications made by anyone other than Northwind or an authorised repairer.

## Making a warranty claim

Contact our support team with your order number and a description of the fault. We may ask for photos or a short video. If the claim is accepted, we will repair or replace the item, or issue a refund if a repair or replacement is not possible.
